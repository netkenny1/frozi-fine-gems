Cancelling, and returning.

Every piece is made after it is ordered, which changes what a return can mean. This page says exactly what applies, and when.

Frozi Fine Gems is not yet trading online. Pieces are quoted and invoiced individually by the atelier; no payment is taken through this website. These terms describe that process.

The licensed entity name, trade licence number and registered Dubai address will be published on this page when the licence is issued, and these documents will be confirmed by UAE counsel before the atelier accepts its first online order.

1. Before the bench begins

You may cancel at any time before work starts, for any reason, and be refunded in full including any deposit. Write to atelier@frozifinegems.com and we will confirm the cancellation the same working day it is read.

2. Once work has started

A commissioned piece is cut and set to your specification and cannot be returned to stock. Once the bench has begun, a cancellation is settled against the work and materials already committed. We will always tell you honestly what stage the piece has reached and what that means in figures before anything is charged.

The invoice states the date work is scheduled to begin, so this point is never a surprise.

3. If a piece is faulty or not as described

This section is not affected by anything above. If a piece arrives damaged, faulty, or materially different from what the invoice describes, tell us within fourteen days of receiving it and we will repair it, remake it, or refund it in full, including return carriage.

Your statutory rights under UAE Federal Law No. 15 of 2020 on Consumer Protection stand alongside this and are not limited by these terms.

4. Fit and sizing

A first resize on a ring or bracelet is carried out at no charge within ninety days of collection, where the design allows it. Some settings — a full-circle band, a pavé shank — can only be resized so far, and we will say so before you order rather than afterwards.

5. What is not a fault

Natural variation in colour, inclusion and character between emeralds is a property of the material, not a defect. So is the ordinary development of a patina on blackened platinum. Neither is a ground for return, though both are covered by aftercare.

6. How a refund is paid

Refunds are made by the same route the payment arrived, in the currency invoiced — UAE dirhams. We issue a refund within fourteen days of agreeing it, or of receiving a returned piece where one is being sent back. How long it then takes to appear depends on your bank.

Please do not send a piece back without telling us first: a returned piece needs to travel insured and declared, and we will arrange that with you.
