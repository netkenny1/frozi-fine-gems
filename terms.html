Legal

Terms of sale and use.

How an order is agreed, what each side undertakes, and the law these terms sit under.

Frozi Fine Gems is not yet trading online. Pieces are quoted and invoiced individually by the atelier; no payment is taken through this website. These terms describe that process.

The licensed entity name, trade licence number and registered Dubai address will be published on this page when the licence is issued, and these documents will be confirmed by UAE counsel before the atelier accepts its first online order.

1. Who you are dealing with

Frozi Fine Gems (“the atelier”, “we”) is a private fine-jewelry house serving clients in Dubai, United Arab Emirates, by appointment. Correspondence: atelier@frozifinegems.com.

2. This website

The drawings, photographs, plate references, text and marks on this site belong to the atelier and may not be copied or reused commercially without written permission. You may browse, save and print pages for your own reference.

The site is offered as it stands. We keep it accurate and available as far as we reasonably can, but we do not warrant uninterrupted access.

3. Prices

Prices are published in UAE dirhams (AED), which is the settlement currency; the atelier invoices in dirhams. Other currencies shown on the site are an indicative courtesy conversion and are not a quotation.

A published price covers the piece as described on its page. A commission, an unusual size, or a change of stone is quoted separately. Any value added tax or duty that applies is shown on the invoice.

4. How an order is formed

Adding a piece to the bag does not charge a card, reserve a stone or form a contract. It prepares a request.

The atelier replies with a written invoice setting out the piece, its size where relevant, the price, the lead time, and the collection or delivery arrangement. A contract is formed when you accept that invoice in writing, not before. We may decline a request, and will say so plainly if we do.

5. Payment

No payment is taken through this website and no card details should ever be sent through the appointment form or by email. Payment terms are set out on the invoice.

Work at the bench begins once the invoice is accepted and any agreed deposit is received. Title in a finished piece passes on payment in full.

6. Made to order

Every piece is made after it is ordered, to a standard six-week bench lead time. Rush work is not offered.

Emeralds and other natural stones vary in colour, inclusion and character from stone to stone. Photographs on this site show a piece of that pattern, not the individual stone you will receive. Reasonable variation of this kind is a property of natural material and is not a fault. Where a specific stone has been selected with you, it is identified on the invoice.

7. Cancelling, returning, delivery

Cancellation and return terms are set out in full in Returns, cancellations & refunds. Collection and delivery are set out in Delivery & collection. Both form part of these terms.

8. Care and aftercare

Resizing, re-oiling and re-polishing of Frozi pieces are offered by appointment for the life of the piece. Aftercare does not cover damage from accident, misuse, or work carried out by another workshop, and repairs of that kind are quoted separately.

9. Responsibility

We are responsible for loss that follows foreseeably from our breaking these terms or failing to use reasonable care. We are not responsible for loss that was not foreseeable, for business losses, or for failures caused by events outside our reasonable control.

Nothing here limits liability for death or personal injury caused by negligence, for fraud, or for anything else that cannot lawfully be limited — including your rights under UAE consumer protection law.

10. Governing law

These terms and any dispute arising from them are governed by the laws of the United Arab Emirates as applied in the Emirate of Dubai, and are subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the Dubai courts.

11. Changes

We may revise these terms. The version that applies to your order is the one published when you accepted your invoice. The date below records the current version.
