Legal

Only what the appointment needs.

This site carries no advertising trackers, no analytics and no mailing list. What little it does handle is set out here in full.

Frozi Fine Gems is not yet trading online. Pieces are quoted and invoiced individually by the atelier; no payment is taken through this website. These terms describe that process.

The licensed entity name, trade licence number and registered Dubai address will be published on this page when the licence is issued, and these documents will be confirmed by UAE counsel before the atelier accepts its first online order.

1. What we collect

Only what you type into the appointment form: your name, your email address, the subject you choose, and your note. If you write to us directly, whatever your message contains.

There is no account to create, no password to store, and no payment card data at any point — the site does not process payments.

2. How it reaches us

The appointment form currently opens a message in your own email program, already filled in, for you to send. Your note therefore travels through your own email provider to atelier@frozifinegems.com, and nothing is submitted to a server belonging to this website.

If that ever changes — if the form begins posting directly to an atelier server — this page will be updated before the change goes live.

3. Why we hold it, and for how long

To reply to you, to prepare the appointment or commission you asked about, and to keep the ledger record that accompanies a finished piece. The lawful basis is your consent in writing to us, and the performance of the contract where an order follows.

Correspondence that does not lead to an order is kept for up to twenty-four months and then deleted. Records connected to an order are kept for as long as UAE commercial and tax law requires.

4. What we do not do

We do not sell, rent or share your details for marketing. There is no mailing list. We do not build a profile of you, and we run no advertising or analytics trackers of any kind.

5. What is stored on your device

The site sets no cookies. It does use your browser’s local storage for three things, all of which stay on your device and are never transmitted to us:

  • frozi-bag-v1 — the pieces in your bag.
  • frozi-currency — the currency you chose to view.
  • frozi-lang — whether you chose English or Arabic.

Clearing your browser’s site data removes all three.

6. Others who necessarily see something

Two services are involved in delivering this site, and both unavoidably receive your IP address as any web server would:

  • GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., United States) hosts and serves these pages.
  • Google Fonts (Google LLC, United States) serves the typefaces the site is set in.

Neither receives anything you type. Because both are outside the UAE, serving this site involves a transfer of that connection data abroad.

7. Your rights

Under UAE Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 on the Protection of Personal Data you may ask us to give you a copy of what we hold about you, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to how we use it, or withdraw a consent you gave. You may also ask us to send it to you in a portable form.

Write to atelier@frozifinegems.com and we will answer within thirty days. If you are not satisfied, you may complain to the UAE Data Office.

8. Security and children

Correspondence is held in the atelier’s own email account under access control. No transmission over the internet is ever completely secure, which is why we ask that card details are never sent to us by email or through the form.

This site is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under eighteen.
