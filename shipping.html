Legal

How a piece reaches you.

By hand at the atelier, or insured and declared to your door. Both are arranged with you before the invoice is issued.

Frozi Fine Gems is not yet trading online. Pieces are quoted and invoiced individually by the atelier; no payment is taken through this website. These terms describe that process.

The licensed entity name, trade licence number and registered Dubai address will be published on this page when the licence is issued, and these documents will be confirmed by UAE counsel before the atelier accepts its first online order.

1. Collection in Dubai

The usual handover is in person, by appointment, at the atelier. The piece is checked with you, fitted where relevant, and handed over with its documentation and its original drawing. There is no charge for collection.

2. Delivery within the UAE

Where you would rather not travel, a piece is sent by insured courier against a signature, fully declared for its invoice value. The cost is shown on the invoice before you accept it. Nothing is left with a neighbour or in a safe place.

3. International delivery

We ship abroad on request, insured and declared, with the export documentation that gemstone shipments require. Because those requirements differ by country, an international shipment is quoted individually rather than from a table.

Any import duty, tax or customs charge in the destination country is payable by you, and is not included in the invoice. Customs may also open a parcel for inspection, which can add days to a delivery.

4. Timing

The six-week bench lead time is quoted from the day the invoice is accepted and any deposit received, and it is time at the bench — carriage is added to it. We tell you when the piece leaves the atelier.

5. Risk and title

Title in a piece passes to you when it has been paid for in full. Risk passes when the piece is handed to you at the atelier or delivered to the address you gave us. A piece in transit is insured by the atelier until it is delivered.

If a package arrives damaged or with its seal broken, please refuse it or note the damage on the courier’s record, and tell us the same day.
